The process

How a week assessment works with Hometails

Four structured touchpoints, designed to understand your dog — not to sell you a package.

01

Behaviour baseline

In the first session, the trainer understands your concerns and assesses your dog’s current training behaviour.

  • Attention & name response
  • Basic commands
  • Impulse control
  • Leash behaviour
  • Home manners
  • Food, play or praise motivation
02

Home practice

The trainer gives you simple exercises to practise between sessions — so we can see whether your dog retains the learning and responds when the trainer isn’t present.

03

Learning-response assessment

In the next session, the trainer checks how your dog is actually learning.

  • Learning speed & retention
  • Motivation
  • Response to distractions
  • Response to the pet parent
  • Repeating taught exercises
04

Training blueprint

In the final session, the trainer assesses progress and creates a personalised training recommendation based on your dog’s individual requirements.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Is the one-week training assessment free?

The assessment is currently complimentary for eligible pet parents in selected serviceable locations. Our team will confirm availability for your area when they call.

How many sessions are included?

The assessment includes three professional at-home training sessions conducted on alternate days during approximately one week.

Why are the sessions on alternate days?

The gap lets you practise trainer-recommended exercises. The trainer can then assess your dog’s learning retention and response during the next visit.

Will my dog be completely trained in one week?

No. The purpose of the assessment is to understand your dog, begin foundation training and determine the right long-term approach. Complete training depends on your dog’s behaviour, age, goals and learning response.

What happens after the assessment?

Your trainer shares the key observations and recommends a personalised training roadmap. You then choose whether you’d like to continue with a Hometails training program.

Will I have to buy a package afterwards?

No. The assessment helps you understand your dog’s training needs. Continuing with a paid Hometails training program is completely optional.

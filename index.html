FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a training session take?

Session duration depends on your dog’s age, attention span and program. Your Hometails trainer will explain the recommended session structure during the demo.

What training methods do you use?

We focus on structured, reward-based training and consistent reinforcement, adapted to your dog’s behaviour and learning pace — no harsh punishment.

Do I have to be present during the training?

Pet-parent participation is encouraged. Consistent cues and reinforcement at home help strengthen progress between trainer visits.

Where do the training sessions take place?

Training is delivered at home, so the trainer can work with your dog in the environment where everyday behaviour actually happens.

My dog listens to the trainer but not to me. Will that improve?

That’s exactly why pet-parent guidance matters. Your trainer shows you how to use the same cues, timing and reinforcement so your dog responds to you too.

Can you train an adult dog, or is it only for puppies?

Adult dogs can absolutely learn. The approach may differ depending on previous habits, temperament and the behaviour you want to improve.

What if my family uses different commands with the dog?

Consistency is important. The trainer helps your household agree on simple cues and routines so the dog gets the same message from everyone.

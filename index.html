FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 7-Day Training Assessment free?

The assessment is currently complimentary for eligible pet parents in selected serviceable locations. Our team confirms availability for your area when they call.

How many sessions are included?

The assessment includes 3 professional at-home training sessions, conducted on alternate days during approximately one week.

Why are the sessions on alternate days?

The gap allows you to practise trainer-recommended exercises. The trainer can then assess your dog’s learning retention and response during the next visit.

Will my dog be completely trained in one week?

No. The purpose of the assessment is to understand your dog, begin foundation training and determine the right long-term approach. Complete training depends on your dog’s behaviour, age, goals and learning response.

What happens after the assessment?

Your trainer shares the key observations and recommends a personalised training roadmap. You can then choose whether to continue with a Hometails training program.

Will I have to buy a package after the assessment?

No. The assessment helps you understand your dog’s training needs. Continuing with a paid Hometails program is entirely optional.

What training methods do you use?

We focus on structured, reward-based training and consistent reinforcement, adapted to your dog’s behaviour and learning pace — no harsh punishment.

Do I have to be present during the sessions?

Pet-parent participation is encouraged. Consistent cues and reinforcement at home help strengthen progress between trainer visits.
